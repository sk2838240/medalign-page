# Image Sources

Every photo on this site comes from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license), under the
Pexels License: free to use commercially, no attribution required, modification allowed.
Attribution is listed here anyway as good practice.

Selection was metadata-driven — each candidate's Pexels `alt`, `description` and `tags`
were checked against per-slot rules before choosing. Global rejects applied during
selection: veterinary/animal subjects, surgery or graphic medical imagery, identifiable
children, visible third-party branding or signage, watermarks, AI-generated imagery,
and dark or low-key lighting that clashes with the brand palette.

Regenerate with `npm run images`. Source URLs live in `scripts/images.manifest.json`.
